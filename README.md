# 🚀 Endless Shift

### Play • Survive • Compete

**Endless Shift** is a fast-paced arcade game collection focused on survival, quick reactions, and high scores.

The goal is simple: **survive as long as possible, collect rewards, avoid obstacles, and beat your best score.**

## 🎮 Games

### 🚀 Endless Shift
Survive, collect coins, avoid obstacles, and keep going as the difficulty increases.

### 🌌 Galaxy Defender
Defend the galaxy, destroy incoming enemies, and try to achieve the highest score.

### 🎯 Target Rush
Test your reaction speed, hit targets quickly, and survive the countdown.

## ✨ Features

- 🎮 Multiple arcade games
- 🏆 Best-score system
- 📈 Increasing difficulty
- 📱 Mobile-friendly controls
- 💾 Local high-score saving
- 🌙 Modern dark gaming interface
- ⚡ Fast and lightweight
- 🔄 New games and improvements planned
